FAQs

Questions founders ask
in the first call.

01 We've already worked with a marketing agency. How is NeonTrumpet different? +
Most agencies optimise for the retainer. We optimise for outcomes. We're led by operators who've sat in the PMM and CMO seat — not account managers. We'll tell you what needs fixing, even if that means referring you elsewhere.
02 Do you offer retainers or fixed-scope engagements? +
Both. Some clients start with a fixed-scope diagnostic and strategy project. Others move straight to ongoing partnership. We'll tell you on the first call which structure makes sense for where you are.
03 What if I only need help with one thing — say, HubSpot setup or LinkedIn? +
We can help with specific projects, but we'll be honest if we think the problem is upstream. Fixing your HubSpot when your messaging is broken doesn't move the needle. We'll tell you straight.
04 How involved do founders need to be? +
Heavily at the start, lighter over time. The first few weeks require your input on positioning, ICP, and company context. After that, we're built to operate with minimal handholding. We don't need babysitting.
05 What happens in the first 30-minute discovery call? +
We ask about where you are, what you've tried, and what's not working. We listen more than we talk. At the end, we tell you what we think you need — including if it's not us. No pitch deck. No pressure.
